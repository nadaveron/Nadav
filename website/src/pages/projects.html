Songs for Empathy

debut albumreleased august 2023

Nadav's debut album gathers his years in Jerusalem and Boston into one statement. Original compositions combine intricate rhythms with warm melodies, drawing on jazz, Latin, classical and Middle Eastern music.

The album is a reflection on the human experience and a call for greater empathy. It moves through compassion, fear and optimism, and draws on the philosophical traditions of Buddhism, Judaism, Sufism and Hinduism.

Playground Trio

a sign of life · 2024jerusalem

An instrumental trio built on friendship. Clarinetist Amitai Mann and guitarist Dor Ben Lulu started as a duo, invited Nadav as a guest at their launch shows, and the chemistry was immediate.

Their debut album A Sign of Life was recorded at HaMazkeka studio in Jerusalem and released in November 2024. Its short, quiet pieces carry a meditative melancholy, written during a time of war and of all three members becoming parents.

Bombaras

balkan & gypsy musicaccordion · sax · guitar · bass · percussion

Bombaras brings a Balkan haflah to the stage: live, loud and made for dancing. The music connects Gypsy and Balkan traditions with the roots of Eastern European Jewish music, songs of wandering, longing and great joy.

The members have played this music for years, including with Gute Gute and The Turbans. Bombaras has performed at FeelBeit Jerusalem, Beit HaConfederatzia, Beit Mizrach-Maarav, TRB Tel Aviv and Goldmund Haifa.

Itai Armon Ensemble

israeli-persian musicsince 2023

Itai Armon is a Jerusalem composer and player of the Persian tar. His ensemble draws on the classical music of Iran, the Middle East and Central Asia, and Nadav's percussion carries pieces such as Saudi Groove, Bayati Shiraz and Hagiga.

David Lavi & Tamar Philosof

החולמים אחרי השמשhebrew songs

Nadav plays percussion with singers David Lavi and Tamar Philosof in HaHolmim Achrei HaShemesh (The Dreamers After the Sun). He recorded the percussion for their songs HaShemesh and Tihye Iti in 2025, using brushes, spoons, shakers, calabash and frame drum to give each song its own colour.

Alma de Bata

soul of the drumpremiered at feelbeit, december 2025

A percussion celebration that puts the drums at the centre of the stage, as a full musical language rather than accompaniment. Three percussionists each bring a tradition: Nadav's Middle Eastern drums, Rony Iwryn's Latin percussion and Itamar Gutman's West African rhythms.

Jewish and Arab musicians meet through rhythm, in pieces written for the ensemble that range from drums alone to the full band.
