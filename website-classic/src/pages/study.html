The method

The Middle Eastern Drum Set

Nadav’s book brings the rhythms of the darbuka and frame drum to the drum kit: students learn the core rhythms by sound and feel, voice them across the kit, and improvise inside them.

Nadav runs an active private studio in Neve Shalom for groups and private students, and teaches at the Kiryat Ono Conservatory and at HaSadna Conservatory in Jerusalem.
