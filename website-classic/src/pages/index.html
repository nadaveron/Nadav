Composer & Curator

Original evenings Nadav composes and directs, such as Mirror and Alma de Bata at FeelBeit, where percussion leads and musicians from different traditions meet.

Session Percussionist

Frame drums, darbuka and riq for singers and ensembles: Persian, Moroccan, Algerian, Balkan and Hebrew song, on stage and in the studio.

Education

Lessons in Middle Eastern percussion and drum set, masterclasses and group workshops, built on Nadav's own method.
About

A percussionist of many traditions

Nadav grew up near Tel Aviv, found the North African roots of his music in Jerusalem, and spent three years as one of Boston's busiest Middle Eastern percussionists. Since coming home in 2023 he has turned fully to percussion: the frame drum, darbuka and riq, played with singers and ensembles across Israel and in productions of his own.
