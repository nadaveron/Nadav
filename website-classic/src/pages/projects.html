Original production · premiered 29 July 2026, Jerusalem

Mirror

Four musicians, two Palestinian and two Jewish, meeting through music. The evening opens with the music that shaped each of them, the sounds of their own homes. They then play the same pieces together, each heard through the other's musical language, and end with music born from the meeting itself, including Nadav's new compositions Mirror I and Mirror II.

Original production · Soul of the Drum · 20 December 2025, Jerusalem

Alma de Bata

An all-original percussion celebration that puts the drums at the centre of the stage. Three percussionists each bring a tradition: Nadav's Middle Eastern drums, Rony Iwryn's Latin percussion and Itamar Gutman's West African rhythms.

Jewish and Arab musicians meet through rhythm, in pieces written for the evening that range from drums alone to the full band.

Songs for Empathy

Original compositions that combine intricate rhythms with warm melodies, drawing on jazz, Latin, classical and Middle Eastern music. A reflection on the human experience, moving through compassion, fear and optimism, and a call for greater empathy.

Balkan & Gypsy party band · percussion, management and booking

Bombaras

A Balkan haflah on stage: live, loud and made for dancing. Bombaras connects Gypsy and Balkan traditions with the roots of Eastern European Jewish music. Its members have played this music for years, including with Gute Gute and The Turbans.

Itai Armon Ensemble
