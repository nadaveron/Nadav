About

“This knowledge and experience is not mine. I’m merely a vessel to transport it to future generations.”Nadav Friedman

Nadav Friedman is a percussionist, composer and educator. On frame drums, darbuka and riq he moves between the rhythms of the Middle East and North Africa, the Balkans, Persia and Latin America, and between the roles of accompanist, bandleader and curator.

Since returning to Israel in 2023 he has turned his attention fully to percussion. At FeelBeit in Jerusalem he creates original productions: Alma de Bata (2025), a celebration of percussion with Rony Iwryn and Itamar Gutman, and Mirror (2026), where Jewish and Palestinian musicians play the music of their homes and then hear it through each other. He accompanies the Israeli-Persian ensemble of tar player Itai Armon, singers Shimon Buskila, Rotem Cohen, David Lavi and Tamar Philosof, and the Balkan party band Bombaras, and plays with Playground Trio.

Nadav was born in Ramat Gan in 1992 and grew up in Hod Hasharon, where he started on drum set at thirteen. After his military service and several years of travel and volunteer work across the Americas, he returned to music in 2014, at the Rimon School and then the Jerusalem Academy of Music and Dance. In Jerusalem he found the North African roots of his music: he was a founding member of HaMadrasa, the Andalusian orchestra led by Hagai Bilitsky, and formed the Soltz Trio with Lior Soltz and Tamir Klein.

During the pandemic he moved to Boston for a Master’s degree at the New England Conservatory. There he became one of the busiest Middle Eastern percussionists in the Northeast, performing with artists including Danilo Pérez, Miguel Zenón, Donny McCaslin, Dave Holland, Ethan Iverson, Jason Moran and Arturo O’Farrill, recording for Hollywood productions, and teaching masterclasses at the Panama Jazz Festival and in Costa Rica. His debut album as a leader, Songs for Empathy, closed that chapter in 2023.

Teaching runs through all of it. Nadav teaches at the Kiryat Ono Conservatory and at HaSadna Conservatory in Jerusalem, and has written his own method, The Middle Eastern Drum Set.

Short bio for programmes and press

Nadav Friedman is an Israeli percussionist, composer and educator. On frame drums, darbuka and riq he accompanies artists including Itai Armon, Shimon Buskila and Rotem Cohen, and creates original productions at FeelBeit Jerusalem, among them Alma de Bata and Mirror. He studied at the Jerusalem Academy of Music and Dance and the New England Conservatory.
