Original productions

FeelBeit terrace, Shift Festival · 29 July 2026

Mirror

Four musicians, two Palestinian and two Jewish, meeting through music. The evening opens with the music that shaped each of them, the sounds of their own homes. They then play those same pieces together, each heard through the other's musical language, and end with music born from the meeting itself, including Nadav's new compositions Mirror I and Mirror II.

Nadav Friedman · percussion, compositions Orel Oshrat · piano Musa Abbasi Luay Abbasi Neomi Maggeni · guest
FeelBeit, Tabaqat Festival · 20 December 2025

Alma de Bata · Soul of the Drum

An all-original percussion celebration that puts the drums at the centre of the stage, as a full musical language rather than accompaniment. Three percussionists each bring a tradition: Nadav's Middle Eastern drums, Rony Iwryn's Latin percussion and Itamar Gutman's West African rhythms.

Jewish and Arab musicians meet through rhythm, in pieces written for the evening that range from drums alone to the full band.

Nadav Friedman · percussion, composition, direction Rony Iwryn · percussion Itamar Gutman · percussion Orel Oshrat · piano Daniel Iwryn · bass, gimbri Omri Porat · guitar, oud
A home stage in Jerusalem

FeelBeit

FeelBeit, the Jerusalem art house where Jewish and Arab artists create together, has become Nadav's home stage. He premiered both of his original productions there, as part of the house's Tabaqat and Shift festivals, and he plays there regularly with other ensembles.

Balkan and Gypsy music · percussion, management & booking

Bombaras

A Balkan haflah on stage: live, loud and made for dancing. Bombaras connects Gypsy and Balkan traditions with the roots of Eastern European Jewish music, and its members have played this music for years, including with Gute Gute and The Turbans. The band has played FeelBeit, Beit HaConfederatzia, Beit Mizrach-Maarav, TRB Tel Aviv and Goldmund Haifa.
